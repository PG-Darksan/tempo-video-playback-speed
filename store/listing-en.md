# Listing text (English)

## Name (taken from the manifest)
Tempo – Video Playback Speed

## Summary (manifest description, max 132 characters)
Change web video playback speed from 0.25× to 16× using the toolbar or a small panel on the video.

## Detailed description (paste into "Description")

Play videos on web pages at any speed from 0.25× to 16×.

■ From the toolbar: every video on the page
• Number input in 0.01 steps, a slider in 0.05 steps, and −/+ buttons in 0.25 steps
• Presets 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×, 16× and a one-click reset to 1×
• Works for embedded videos (iframes) too
• The toolbar badge shows the actual speed of the videos in that tab

■ On the video: just that video
• −, current speed (press for 1×), + and details (number input, slider, presets)
• Collapse it into a small, faint icon that stays out of the way
• Fully usable with the keyboard

■ Convenient
• "Remember speed" applies your last speed to newly opened videos
• Turning it OFF restores only the videos the extension changed (speeds set by the site are kept)
• Turn off "Show on videos" to use the toolbar only
• Available in English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Hindi and Arabic

■ Privacy
• Settings are stored only in this browser
• No URLs, browsing history or video information is stored or sent anywhere
• No account, no ads, no analytics

■ Notes
• It does not work on browser settings pages or extension store pages
• Some sites or special players may block speed changes
• To use it with video files on your computer, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" in the extension's details
• Reload tabs that were already open before installing
